## MAKEUP OF A HEALTHY SMILE

* * *

Carson Dental Designs offers family dentistry to address oral health at every stage of life. Family dentistry focuses on oral hygiene and overall health of teeth from children to adults.  Dr. West has a gentle approach helping children develop positive associations with regular dental checkups and to build good oral hygiene habits for life. We help your children maintain proper dental health and prevent tooth decay, which can save both your health and your finances in the future. Dr. West also spends the time to explain the problems and to not only tell you, but show you where and what they are in detail.

## A GREAT LOOKING SMILE

* * *

## THE HEALTH BENEFITS

* * *

Cosmetic dentistry is a specialty at Carson Dental Designs. We design our cosmetic dentistry procedures around creating a positive change to your teeth and to your smile. Our skilled, experienced cosmetic dentists and staff can help enhance your appearance and smile, which often can improve your self-esteem and confidence. We employ all of the latest cosmetic dentistry procedures necessary to address functional and aesthetic patient concerns.

* * *

We believe that aesthetic dentistry complements the overall general and oral health of the patient.  We strive for minimally invasive treatment protocols when and ensure they are consistent with the long-term health needs of our patients. Our state-of-the-art technology and materials allow us to provide dentistry services that are predictable and deliver long-lasting results. The positive changes that we can make to your smile, through a wide variety of procedures, techniques, and the newest materials, can be life changing.

 ![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%200%200'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

* * *

#### VENEERS

Veneers offer an amazing change to the shape and color of your teeth with minimal removal of tooth surface. Some people only need a few veneers to fix previously broken teeth with old discolored fillings. Others with serious discolorations and wear may need more attention. We offer a consultation to determine what is best for you.

* * *

#### TMJ THERAPY

Many broken teeth are caused from parafunctioning also known as clenching and grinding your teeth. This is also often times related to sleep breathing disorders caused from mouth breathing. So merely fixing a broken tooth is not fixing the source of why you broke your tooth. Dr. West wants to fix problems and not only treat symptoms.

* * *

#### WHITENING

Teeth become stained and discolored due to drinking coffee, wine, and tea. Our whitening systems greatly reduce sensitivity while making your teeth whiter. How white depends on a number of factors, call to find out if whitening is the right option for you.

* * *

#### WHITE FILLINGS

We DO NOT use amalgam or silver fillings. We only use white composite resin fillings where necessary. These fillings are more esthetic and have no metal, thus providing a healthier dental repair.

* * *

#### CROWNS

When you’ve had a trauma or large previous restoration fail or break, you will need a crown to protect the tooth. Our crowns are made of porcelain and are natural looking and esthetic. Whether you need a crown on your front tooth or in the back we make it as natural looking as possible with as minimal tooth removal as necessary to save your teeth. A crown makes your natural tooth stronger and protected.

## OUR 3D CONEBEAM TECHNOLOGY

* * *

With all implants, every patient needs a 3D scan to properly plan their implant.  This scan shows so much more any regular X ray.  This is an amazing new technology we’re proud to offer and allows us the ability to provide a much more accurate and precise result.  Also to see other areas of the mouth and possibly diagnose something hiding or in other areas.

* * *

Implants are now used for tooth replacement, when before only bridges and partial dentures were available.  If a tooth becomes too severely damaged to repair due to decay, trauma or improper care a decision may be made to employ one of the various implant options.  These are a more involved treatment in comparison to a cavity, filling or root canal treatment but allow you to retain quality dental health.

* * *

#### SINGLE IMPLANTS

When you have a single missing tooth from birth or you had to have your tooth extracted. An implant is usually the best option to give you back your tooth and keep balanced chewing. When you lose a tooth, it puts more pressure on all of your other teeth to do more work. By replacing a missing tooth we take the strain away from the surrounding teeth and restore a more healthy bite. Implants are typically used for 1-3 missing teeth in the same area.

* * *

#### IMPLANT BRIDGES

If you are missing more than a few teeth in one area then an implant bridge is a good idea to minimize your costs and maximize your chewing capability. We will discuss options during your exam and work with you to ensure the best overall result.

* * *

#### NON-METAL IMPLANTS

Some people have allergies to metal, even medical grade titanium can cause issues with their immune system. Titanium implants have been the standard for many years, but can cause issues for those with metal allergies. We’re happy to offer another option of a metal free implant. Call and schedule a consultation with Dr. West to find out if this the right choice for you.

* * *

#### HYBRID IMPLANTS

When you have many broken and hopeless teeth or you have had a denture that just isn’t right. A hybrid can be used and sits on multiple implants and is secured into position and is not removable. The teeth are very natural looking and very strong, unlike your denture that can break if you drop it. This is the closest and most realistic to natural teeth. It is a more expensive option but when it comes to how you look, feel and eat; eating and functioning like real teeth is well worth the cost.

* * *

#### LOCATOR IMPLANTS

When you have a denture that is loose, locator implants can help stabilize your denture and make it tighter. These have a male and female attachment that locks your denture into place providing stability so you can eat a wider variety of foods.

### Where it all started

**Dr. West** is a Reno native.  He grew up in the North Valleys/Cold Springs area.  He graduated from Hug High School and went directly into the United States Air Force.  He served honorably as an F-15E crew chief with a medal serving in Southwest Asia during Desert Watch.   After his tour of duty completed, Dr. West returned home and attended University of Nevada, Reno.  After graduating with his bachelor’s degree in Biology, he attended the University of Nevada, Las Vegas School of Dental Medicine.  Dr. West is proud to have returned to Northern Nevada, while previously serving the communities of Las Vegas, Elko, Carson City and now Reno.

### Outside the office

Dr. West enjoys traveling, going to Lake Tahoe, and any outdoor activity. In his spare time he loves watching movies and also reading, he enjoys reading fantasy fiction, mystery, spy, and some nonfiction.   He loves to discuss books with his patients and often shares recommendations back and forth.  He has a special soft spot for Sherlock Holmes mysteries.

### There for you

Dr. West believes the most important function of the dental office is the patient experience.  He and the staff strive to be thorough and to treat the patient completely as a whole.  There are many choices when deciding on any caregiver.  The most important task is finding someone who listens and understands the patient and their needs.   Dr. West believes this is only achieved through educating his patients and helping them to overcome their fears.

### Dr. West Gives Back

One of the ways Dr. West gives back is as a proud member of the [Adopt A Vet Program](http://www.adoptavetdental.com/) helping Vets in need. Dr. West donates full treatment as needed to help our Vets begin the healing process from the inside out.

### From the Adopt A Vet Website:

Launched in 2010, the Adopt a Vet Dental Program (AAVD) provides pro bono dental care to Northern Nevada’s low-income veterans who are in critical need. Although most of these veterans fought bravely for us in the front lines, they do not qualify for treatment at the VA Dental Clinic. What’s more, they do not have dental insurance, and average a humble income of $900 a month. AAVD merged with Northern Nevada Dental Health in 2013 with the goal of combining our efforts and continuing to improve the lives of veterans in need of dental care. In the four years of our programs existence, we have helped over 540 Veterans, receiving over $1.6 Million in dental care services by our pro bono providers.

### Why not use the VA Dental Clinic?

VA Dental policies are so restrictive, only about 5% of our local veterans qualify for care. With over 30,000 local veterans this leaves a shocking majority of them without dental care.

### Who’s it for?

The goal of our program is to help low-income veterans who simply cannot afford to see a dentist. Because they lack dental treatment, many face poor overall health, self-esteem issues, or cannot secure work. Most of these veterans do not have a family or support system, which forces them to rely on help from their community.
