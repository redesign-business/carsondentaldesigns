## Same Day & Emergency Care

- Same-day services available for dental emergencies.
- Emergency dental appointments offered for all patients, whether first visit or returning.
- Maintains open appointments to accommodate urgent dental care during regular office hours; in many cases same day dentistry is available.
- Handles toothaches, chipped teeth, emergency root canal therapy, trauma, lost fillings, knocked-out teeth, extruded teeth, lost crowns, abscesses, and soft-tissue injuries.

## Technology & Care Standards

- 3D Conebeam technology: every implant patient receives a 3D scan for accurate and precise planning.
- State-of-the-art technology and materials delivering predictable, long-lasting results with minimally invasive treatment protocols.
- Uses only non-metal materials for white fillings and porcelain crowns; white composite resin fillings instead of amalgam or silver fillings.
- Offers metal-free (non-titanium) implant option for patients with metal allergies.
- Employs TMJ therapy and sleep appliances addressing sleep breathing disorders, clenching, grinding, and other parafunctional habits.
- Works hand in hand with local orthodontists to provide quality dental care while patients wear braces.
- Offers in-house insurance program, which patients report saving money on.
